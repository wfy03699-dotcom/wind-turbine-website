Product Overview

Explore a practical starting point for small-scale renewable wind energy. This horizontal axis wind turbine has a rated power of 400W and a maximum power of 450W, with 12V, 24V or 48V DC voltage options to discuss for your system.

Fiberglass nylon blades and an aluminum alloy body provide durable construction in a compact format. The specified generator is a 3-phase AC permanent magnet generator, with electromagnetic control. Confirm the controller, rectification, battery compatibility and supporting equipment for your chosen configuration before installation.

Wind energy is site-dependent. Rated power is specified at 11 m/s; it is not a guarantee of continuous output. Assess local wind conditions, mounting, clearances and local requirements with a qualified installer.
